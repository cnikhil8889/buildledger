Your records stay yours.

BuildLedger Android 0.3.0 lets you choose phone storage or private BuildLedger Cloud after signing in. Available sign-in providers depend on the developer configuration. This source distribution needs backend setup before sign-in.

Sign-in and phone storage

Google, Apple ID or Microsoft sign-in is processed through Supabase Auth and your system browser. Profile/account information and encrypted native session tokens support your login. Passwords and tokens are not entered into the app interface. Login does not connect Google Drive, iCloud Drive or OneDrive. Phone choice keeps project records on this device unless you export them.

Private cloud folder

Cloud choice creates an account-specific BuildLedger folder in private application storage. Your builder profile, projects, buyer/financial records and document bytes are uploaded as complete snapshots while the app is open or resumed. A phone copy supports offline work. Pending uploads, errors and conflicts are shown in Storage. Cloud records are access controlled; no end-to-end encryption is claimed. The earlier private website is a separate workspace.

Shared company workspaces

Creating or joining a company stores company records online, independently of your personal phone/cloud storage choice. Owners and office staff can access company projects, member names/emails, site attendance, wage rates/payments, materials and work reports. Workers can access assigned projects, their own tasks, attendance and wages, and shared progress reports for assigned sites. The owner controls invitations and membership roles. Office staff manage project assignments and site records. Activity records identify who made company changes.

Invitations are restricted to the invited person's verified sign-in email. The owner chooses how to share the invitation code; the app sends no invitation emails or messages automatically. Company screens require an internet connection and keep their displayed records in memory rather than a persistent offline database. Signing out or uninstalling does not remove the company's cloud history. Disabling a member prevents future access but cannot remove reports they previously exported.

Device protection and exports

Local metadata and tokens are encrypted with Android Keystore. Imported document bytes stay in app-private storage. A configured screen lock protects app access on launch and after background inactivity. Selected external viewers get read-only access to the document you open. Android file providers handle imports, backups and report exports. Personal backup ZIPs are not encrypted: choose a private destination. They include the personal workspace and files, not shared company records. Company CSV exports contain the records visible to your role; wage totals include all approved attendance and recorded payments while history lists contain recent entries. Company CSV is a report, not a restorable company backup.

Delete your data

Delete phone copy removes the current account's local workspace and preserves its cloud records. Permanent account/cloud deletion is disabled in this private test build; its endpoint has not been deployed. Other devices' offline copies and exported backups/reports must be removed separately. Uninstalling removes app-private phone data. Earlier phone records are preserved separately until you delete them from that workspace.

Permissions and release information

Internet access supports configured login/cloud sync. No ads, analytics, broad storage, location, contacts or camera permissions are included. Identity providers, Supabase and chosen file/viewing services apply their own terms.

The developer's legal identity, support contact, hosting/retention details, public privacy policy and deletion-request URL must be finalized before a public Play release. The backend and identity providers must be configured for each deployment. Account deletion and the public release information remain unfinished.
