# Store listing draft

App name: BuildLedger

Category suggestion: Business

Short description:
Projects, documents, payments and buyer records for Indian builders.

Full description:
BuildLedger helps builders keep the details behind every construction project organized.

Create your builder profile and manage projects by name and year. Keep legal records, title history, civil and architectural drawings, buyer agreements, sale documents, budgets and payment receipts with the right project.

Follow every rupee with a clear record of contractor bills, materials, part payments and outstanding balances. Track workers, paint, painters, steel, cement, bricks, granite, tiles, electrical labour, wiring, legal fees and additional costs.

Add buyers, record agreement values, capture collections and see remaining balances. Review project budgets and financial summaries, save CSV reports, or use Android's print/save-to-PDF flow.

Sign in with Google, Apple or Microsoft, then choose phone storage or your private BuildLedger Cloud folder. Keep working with the phone copy offline; cloud accounts sync while the app is open and connected. See pending uploads and resolve conflicts without silently overwriting newer records.

Import documents through Android's file picker and export complete backups to available providers, including Google Drive. Login does not connect a personal cloud drive. Backups contain readable records/files, so choose a private destination.

The development document checklist is an organizational aid. Requirements vary by state, project and authority; uploaded files are not a determination of legal compliance.
